Hi, my name is Kostis and I'm a software engineer at Skroutz.gr. Besides my life as a developer I enjoy photography. I am self-taught and started my involvement with photography in 2012. I like urban and sub-urban landscapes which emit the feeling of loneliness, abandonment and curiosity. Nowadays I shoot mostly film - 6×7 medium format and 35mm.

If you want to get in touch, send me an e-mail or follow me on Instagram.
