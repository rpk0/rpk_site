Hi, my name is Kostis and I'm a software engineer at Skroutz.gr. Besides my life as a developer I enjoy photography. I am self-taught and started my involvement with photography in 2012. I like urban and sub-urban landscapes which emit the feeling of loneliness, abandonment and curiosity. Nowadays I shoot mostly film because I don't have much time for post processing my digital images. This site began as a fork of holman/left but Vangelis took it to another level with his amazing design.

If you want to get in touch, send me an e-mail or follow me on Instagram.
